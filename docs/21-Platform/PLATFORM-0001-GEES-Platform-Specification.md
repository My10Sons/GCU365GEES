# GEES Platform Specification

## Executive Summary

The GEES Platform is an AI-native Engineering Governance Platform.

Its purpose is to manage engineering knowledge, enforce governance, maintain traceability, assist engineers through AI, and provide a single authoritative source for all engineering artifacts.

The platform is not a project management system.

The platform is not a source code repository.

The platform is the enterprise engineering knowledge platform.

---

# Vision

Create the world's most comprehensive engineering governance platform where humans and AI collaborate using governed engineering knowledge.

---

# Platform Objectives

The platform SHALL:

- Manage engineering artifacts.
- Manage engineering relationships.
- Validate engineering quality.
- Enforce governance.
- Support AI agents.
- Generate engineering documents.
- Maintain traceability.
- Support engineering search.
- Produce engineering analytics.

---

# Core Platform Modules

The platform SHALL consist of the following modules.

## Artifact Repository

Stores all governed engineering artifacts.

Capabilities:

- Versioning
- Metadata
- Ownership
- Search
- Classification

---

## Knowledge Graph

Maintains semantic relationships between artifacts.

Supports:

- Impact Analysis
- Dependency Analysis
- AI Reasoning
- Traceability
- Discovery

---

## Traceability Engine

Maintains:

- Forward Traceability
- Backward Traceability
- Coverage Analysis
- Impact Analysis

---

## Validation Engine

Validates:

- Metadata
- Templates
- References
- Identifiers
- Lifecycle
- Quality Rules

---

## AI Collaboration Engine

Coordinates AI development agents.

Capabilities:

- Artifact Generation
- Review Assistance
- Gap Detection
- Traceability Suggestions
- Quality Suggestions
- Engineering Recommendations

---

## Search Engine

Supports:

- Semantic Search
- Full Text Search
- Graph Search
- Metadata Search
- Similarity Search

---

## Governance Engine

Manages:

- Reviews
- Approvals
- Compliance
- Policies
- Audits

---

## Metrics Engine

Produces:

- Engineering KPIs
- Artifact Quality
- Coverage
- Compliance
- Maturity
- AI Utilization

---

## Template Engine

Provides:

- Document Templates
- Artifact Templates
- Checklists
- Standard Sections

---

## Reporting Engine

Produces:

- Engineering Dashboards
- Compliance Reports
- Audit Reports
- Maturity Reports
- Traceability Reports

The platform SHALL enforce:

- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

# Future Capabilities

Future versions MAY include:

- Automatic standards generation
- Automatic architecture review
- Automatic test generation
- Automatic compliance assessment
- AI-driven impact analysis

# AI Implementation Contract

AI agents SHALL:

- Consume approved artifacts only.
- Preserve traceability.
- Follow lifecycle rules.
- Respect ownership.
- Never approve artifacts.
- Explain recommendations.
